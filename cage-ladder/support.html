Support for Cage Ladder

Need help or want to share feedback? Email Onur Onarici at onuronarici@gmail.com. Please tell us what happened and which iPhone and which iOS version you use. You do not need to send personal information to describe a game issue.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock all caves?

The first three caves are free. Tap the “Full game” row on the Caves screen, or “Unlock Full Game” in the pause menu, then follow the steps. The purchase is a single payment through the App Store. There are no subscriptions, coins or ads. You need an internet connection to buy.

I bought the unlock before. How do I get it back?

Tap “Restore Purchases” on the unlock screen, or in the pause menu. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. You need an internet connection. If the game says that no earlier purchase was found, check that you are signed in to the right Apple Account, then try again. If it still fails, email us.

Can you refund a purchase?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund a purchase ourselves.

Can you restore lost progress?

Progress is saved only on your device. The game has no account or online sync. If the local data is gone, we cannot recover it for you. A purchase is not lost with it: use “Restore Purchases” after you reinstall.

Why can't I hear sound?

Check your device's volume and any sound control in the game. If sound still does not work, please email us.

What happens if I reinstall the game?

Deleting the game removes its locally saved progress and settings. A new install starts without that data. Your purchase stays with your Apple Account, so you can restore it.
