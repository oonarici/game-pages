Privacy Policy for Cage Ladder

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Cage Ladder is an offline, single-player game published by Onur Onarici. It has no account, no ads, and no analytics. It has one optional in-app purchase, “Unlock All Caves”, which opens the caves after the first three.

Information on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device. This information stays there. Deleting the game removes its locally saved progress and settings. We cannot recover them for you.

The in-app purchase

The purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details, and the game does not send them anywhere. After you buy or restore the purchase, the game keeps the unlocked state on your device, so it works offline. Buying or restoring needs an internet connection, because the App Store must confirm it.

Information we collect

The game does not collect or send your personal information or game data to us or to third parties. It does not track you.

If you choose to email us for support, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use that information only to reply to you and delete support messages when they are no longer needed.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Parents and guardians can contact us with questions about this policy.

Changes to this policy

If the game's privacy practices change, we will update this page and its date. Please check this page for the current policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Onur Onarici at onuronarici@gmail.com.
