Privacy Policy for C++ Interview Prep

Last updated: 17 November 2026

C++ Interview Prep is an offline flash-card app for C++ interview practice, published by Onur Onarici. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. It has one optional in-app purchase, “Full Access”, which unlocks every level after the first two free levels in each category.

Information on your device

The app saves your study progress, review schedule, bookmarks and settings on your device. This information stays there. Deleting the app removes it, and we cannot recover it for you.

The in-app purchase

The purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details. After you buy or restore the purchase, the app keeps the unlocked state on your device, so it works offline. Buying or restoring needs an internet connection, because the App Store must confirm it.

Information we collect

The app does not collect or send your personal information or study data to us or to third parties. It does not track you.

If you choose to email us, for example with “Report a mistake”, we receive your email address, the card ID the app adds to the message, and anything you write. We use this only to reply to you and to correct cards, and we delete messages when they are no longer needed.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Parents and guardians can contact us with questions about this policy.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, we will update this page and its date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Onur Onarici at onuronarici@gmail.com.
