Support for C++ Interview Prep

Need help, found a wrong answer, or want to share feedback? Email Onur Onarici at onuronarici@gmail.com. For a card mistake, the quickest way is “Report a mistake” on the card: it adds the card ID for you. A link to a source (cppreference, the C++ standard or the C++ Core Guidelines) helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

How are the cards checked?

Every code card is compiled and run with Clang and GCC, in C++17 and C++20, with and without optimisation, and with sanitizers. Every answer is also checked by an independent AI reviewer that first answers the question without seeing the card, and every source quote is checked against its page. No human expert reviews the cards, so mistakes are possible. Please report them.

What is free, and what does Full Access unlock?

The first two levels of each category (Junior, Mid/Senior and Lead) are free. Full Access is a single payment through the App Store that unlocks every level, including content added in future updates. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

I bought Full Access before. How do I get it back?

Tap “Restore Purchases” on the Unlock screen or in Settings, using the same Apple Account that made the purchase. You need an internet connection. If no earlier purchase is found, check that you are signed in to the right Apple Account, then try again. If it still fails, email us.

Can you refund a purchase?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund a purchase ourselves.

Can you restore lost progress?

Progress is saved only on your device. The app has no account or online sync, so if the data is gone we cannot recover it. Your purchase is not lost: use “Restore Purchases” after you reinstall.

A level is locked. How do I open it?

Clear at least 80% of the cards in the previous level, or use “Skip ahead” on the level path. Browse always lets you read the topics.
